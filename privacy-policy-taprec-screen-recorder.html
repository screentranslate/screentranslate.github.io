Privacy Policy

TapRec: Screen Recorder

Last Updated: November 3, 2026
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TapRec: Screen Recorder accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

1 Introduction

TapRec: Screen Recorder is an Android application that allows users to record their device screens and capture screenshots easily.

The application provides the following features:

  • Record videos of your device screen.
  • Capture screenshots.
  • Save and manage recorded videos and screenshots.
  • Customize screen recording settings.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding user privacy and data protection.

2 Permissions and Access

To provide its features, TapRec may request the following permissions:

Screen Recording (Media Projection)

The application uses Android's MediaProjection API to capture screen content. Users must grant permission through the Android system prompt before screen recording can begin.

Photos, Videos and Storage

The application may access relevant media permissions to save, display, and manage videos and screenshots created by the application.

Microphone (If Enabled)

If users choose to record their voice during screen recording, the application may request microphone permission. Microphone access is used only for the corresponding recording feature.

Notifications

The application may use notifications to display recording status and other relevant information.

3 Information Collection and Use

TapRec does not require users to create an account to use its main screen recording and screenshot features.

Videos and screenshots created using the application are stored on the user's device.

We do not operate a server to collect or store users' screen recordings or screenshots. We do not intentionally upload this content to our own servers.

We do not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

4 Advertising and Third-Party Services

TapRec may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

These third-party services may collect or process certain information, depending on their services and applicable settings, including:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • Device information and operating system details.
  • Advertising interactions and performance data.
  • Diagnostic and analytics information.

This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and improve services.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

5 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to respect user privacy and protect information processed by the application.

Videos and screenshots saved on your device are managed through Android's storage system and applicable device permissions.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices and the files stored on them.

6 Data Sharing

We do not share users' screen recordings or screenshots with our servers or third parties.

However, integrated third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers (if applicable), may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

7 User Choices and Control

Users can:

  • Grant or deny permissions requested by Android.
  • Start or stop screen recording at any time.
  • Choose what content to capture or record.
  • Delete recorded videos and screenshots.
  • Revoke previously granted permissions through device settings.

Denying certain permissions may prevent the corresponding application features from working.

8 Children's Privacy

TapRec does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation in accordance with applicable laws.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in application features, third-party services, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
